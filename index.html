About Me

Driving scalable lab innovation through automation, lean manufacturing, and frugal experimentation.

I’m a scientist and engineer drawn to the art of building elegant, scalable systems out of complex scientific workflows. With a foundation in materials research, lab automation, and process development, I help teams streamline operations and bring structure to the messiness of discovery.

My work blends hands-on lab expertise with automation tools like Hamilton Venus, Python, machine learning, and Six Sigma methodologies—turning redundancy into efficiency, and data into direction. I’ve developed patented water-soluble fluorescent dyes and conducted research across nanoparticle synthesis, MOF catalysis, and solar-active films, all while staying grounded in analytical techniques like UV/Vis, GPC, SEM, XRD, and GC-MS.

I believe that the best systems—whether inherited or new—are those designed with transferability, traceability, and continuous improvement in mind. That means starting with well-defined operational and analytical goals, and designing with both humans and scale in mind.

I’m most at home in environments where curiosity is valued, cross-functional collaboration is the norm, and failure is treated as a fertile ground for learning. I thrive on helping teams do more with less—stretching resources through frugal experimentation and creative problem-solving before reaching for expensive solutions.

If the lab is a machine, I’m here to make it run smoother, smarter, and more sustainably.
